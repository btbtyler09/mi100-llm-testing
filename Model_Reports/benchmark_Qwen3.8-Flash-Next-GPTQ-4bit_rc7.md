## Recommendations

- Use c=2 (interactive-optimal by TTFT/TPOT p99; per-user 65.0 tok/s) for interactive UX.
- Cap interactive concurrency at c=2 by default and use queueing/backpressure when demand exceeds it, keeping TTFT and TPOT p99 near the observed interactive-optimal operating point.
- Treat increases in p99 TPOT or TTFT as the stopping signal for interactive scaling: once either begins to degrade, hold concurrency steady and buffer excess interactive requests instead of admitting more.

- Allow batch/offline-only experiments above c=2 only as an explicitly labeled throughput trade-off, and only when aggregate throughput gain is worth degraded batch TTFT/TPOT without harming protected interactive pools.
- Scale interactive capacity by adding parallel serving lanes across the available devices rather than exceeding c=2 on a single lane, then re-measure per-user tok/s and p99 metrics before shifting more traffic.
