## Recommendations

- Use c=2 (interactive-optimal by TTFT/TPOT p99; per-user 48.6 tok/s) for interactive UX.

- Rerun the same TTFT/TPOT/throughput regression after scheduler, model, or infrastructure changes, and gate promotion on no worsening of the c=2 interactive baseline.
