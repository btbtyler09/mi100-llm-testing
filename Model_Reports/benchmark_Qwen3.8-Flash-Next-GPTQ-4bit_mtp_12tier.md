## Recommendations

- Use c=2 (interactive-optimal by TTFT/TPOT p99; per-user 91.0 tok/s) for interactive UX.
- Keep interactive deployments at or near c=2 when user-visible responsiveness is the primary goal, because this point was identified as best for TTFT/TPOT p99 and per-user generation rate.
- Separate interactive traffic from batch or offline workloads so background throughput does not degrade interactive p99 TTFT/TPOT.
- If batch/offline throughput must be prioritized, treat higher concurrency as a clear latency trade-off and confirm that relaxed per-user TTFT/TPOT requirements are acceptable before routing such jobs there.
- Avoid raising interactive concurrency beyond c=2 solely to increase aggregate throughput unless monitoring shows that per-user p99 TTFT/TPOT remains within acceptable interactive bounds.
- Since GPU information is not available, validate concurrency limits through environment-specific load testing and monitor TTFT/TPOT p99 plus throughput under representative mixed traffic before shifting more users away from c=2.
