# TraceLens on MI100 (gfx908) — MI100 addendum

Read this before reference.md on a gfx908 host. It overrides conflicting guidance. Status tags: **[measured]** on our
cards, **[spec]** vendor sheet, **[unverified]** not tested here.

## Platform choice (Step 0)

MI100 is not in the platform menu (MI300X…MI355X) and TraceLens ships no `MI100.json`. Do **not** pick MI300X: its
roofline is ~4x HBM and ~7x MFMA ours, so every `% of roofline` would read near zero. Create
`$TL_EXTENSION/MI100.json` (reference.md searches `TL_EXTENSION` for `<platform>.json` first) using the constants
below, and answer "MI100" at the prompt.

Shipped arch files carry *max achievable* rates, not spec peaks (MI300X.json: fp16 654 vs 1307 spec). Our MI100.json
uses **sustained rates at the stock 290 W cap, all four cards loaded at once** (2026-10-08, torch 2.10.0+rocm7.0,
rocBLAS via `torch.matmul` 8192², `torch._int_mm` 8192², 512 MiB read/copy; 30 s warm, 10 s timed per op; fans ramped
because GPU0 is loaded; hottest junction 82 °C, no throttle stops):

```json
{"name": "MI100", "mem_bw_gbps": 926, "memory_gb": 32,
 "max_achievable_tflops": {"matrix_fp16": 123.7, "matrix_bf16": 65.8, "matrix_fp32": 34.3, "matrix_int8": 88.0,
                           "vector_fp16": 46.1, "vector_bf16": 23.1, "vector_fp32": 23.1, "vector_fp64": 11.5}}
```

| field | 290 W (stock) | spec | note |
|---|---|---|---|
| `mem_bw_gbps` | **926** read (952 copy, r+w) | 1228.8 | per card 836-1012; HBM is not power-bound (200 W: 961) |
| `matrix_fp16` | **123.7** | 184.6 | 67% of spec; see "gap to spec" below |
| `matrix_bf16` | **65.8** | 92.3 | bf16 = half-rate MFMA on CDNA1 (fp16 expert GEMMs for prefill are a shipped lever) |
| `matrix_fp32` | **34.3** | 46.1 | |
| `matrix_int8` | **88.0** | 184.6 | `torch._int_mm`; below fp16 here - do not assume int8 GEMM beats fp16 on gfx908 without a kernel bench |
| `vector_*` | - | spec | not measured |
| no `matrix_fp8` | - | - | gfx908 has no FP8/FP4 hardware; the key is absent so "Peak MAF (FP8)" is skipped |

|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 100 W | 41.6 | 22.8 | 12.3 | 31.8 | 379 | 300 | 55 °C |
| 150 W | 74.2 | 40.5 | 21.7 | 59.7 | 722 | 495-731 | 64 °C |

**Gap to the 184.6 TFLOPS spec is clock, not kernel efficiency** (290 W peak hunt on GPU0, 2026-10-08):
- rocBLAS fp16 runs at **92-97% of the clock-scaled peak** (184.6 x sclk/1502) on square shapes from 8192 to 16384. Under load at 290 W the SMU holds sclk at 1029-1189 MHz, power-limited (`THROTTLED`), never the 1502 MHz the spec assumes. The best sustained number is 123.8 TF (12288²). A cold first call hits 132 TF before the clock settles.
- **hipBLASLt is unusable on gfx908**: fp16 9-21 TF, bf16 8.8 TF on every shape (~10% of spec). Keep rocBLAS: don't set `preferred_blas_library("cublaslt")` or `TORCH_BLAS_PREFER_HIPBLASLT` on MI100.
- `allow_fp16/bf16_reduced_precision_reduction` changes nothing (same kernels).
- Shape outlier: an M=4096, N=21504, K=5376 fp16 GEMM (Gemma-4 31B up-projection) reaches only 79.6 TF (56% of clock-scaled). bf16 at the same shape is fine (65 TF), so it's a rocBLAS fp16 tuning gap for that shape. Tune it, or check it before blaming the model.

Relative anchors we do have: a 30B dense GPTQ-4bit model's prefill ran GEMM-bound at ~67% of the fp16 spec; the Granite SFT step at ~20% of
capped bf16 peak.

## Verified run on our trace [measured, 2026-10-07]

TraceLens @17a78491 (pip install from the AMD-AGI/TraceLens checkout, Python 3.12 venv, CPU only),
`TraceLens_generate_perf_report_pytorch_inference --gpu_arch_json_path MI100.json --group_by_parent_module
--enable_pseudo_ops --group_by_num_kernels --include_call_stack` on a Flash-Next (TP=4, MTP off) decode trace, rank 0:
1m54s on CPU, 290,237 kernels, count/detail checks PASS. GPU timeline: busy 93.6%, idle 6.4%, exposed comm 0.5%,
exposed memcpy 2.1%.

**Limit on our fork:** only **1.1% of kernel time** had a TraceLens perf model (plain aten ops: copy_, add, clamp_min_).
Our decode kernels are JIT HIP extensions inside CUDA graphs; they appear as synthetic `hipGraphLaunch-><kernel>` ops in
category `other` (82% of time under `hipModuleLaunchKernel`) with no roofline model. So on our stack use TraceLens for the
timeline split, top-kernel ranking and idle analysis, and do roofline by hand (bytes moved / measured time vs 1042 GB/s,
which is how our GEMVs were sized). The GEMM/SDPA/MoE subagents will find little to model. Graph-capture mode
(`--capture_folder`) was not tried.

Useful measured anchors for "is this near the floor?":
- bf16 GEMV (vLLM `wvSplitK`, decode M=1): 660–985 GB/s on real shapes ⇒ 65–98% of HBM.
- Exllama GPTQ GEMV at M=1: 589–803 GB/s on big shapes (near roofline); our HIP W8/W4A8 GEMVs: 78–97% of size-matched floors.
- Triton nibble (W4) GEMV codegen on gfx908: ~80 GB/s — a Triton W4 decode kernel showing ~7% of roofline is the codegen, not the trace.
- One custom all-reduce at TP=4: 7.8 µs stock vs 5.3 µs push-AR (xGMI). Comm is ≤ ~5% of a decode step on our serves.

## Trace-quality caveats specific to gfx908 (Step 2 gate)

- **The torch/kineto profiler inflates every small kernel** (~1.3 µs real → ~3.75 µs traced inside a CUDA graph; a
  1,900-kernel decode step shows ~4 ms of fake time, profiled step ~25% slower). Treat per-kernel times under ~30 µs
  as unreliable; the cpu-idle and kernel-fusion subagents will over-rank "launch floor" fusions. Size host idle with
  `py-spy record --nonblocking -f raw` on the worker plus the un-profiled step period instead.
- Profile **decode-only windows**. A profile that includes prefill once showed NCCL at +1.9 ms/step; decode-only it was +0.34.
- Boot-to-boot noise is 0.3–0.4 ms/step: comparative mode needs ≥3 boots per arm or an in-process A/B before a
  finding below ~0.5 ms/step is real.

## Capturing a trace on our vLLM fork (works on rc10 / -q38fn-mtp)

```bash
# serve with the torch profiler wired to a mounted dir
docker run ... -v /path/to/prof:/prof <image> vllm serve ... \
  --profiler-config '{"profiler":"torch","torch_profiler_dir":"/prof"}'
curl -X POST localhost:8000/start_profile   # then a c=1 request of ~64-256 tokens
curl -X POST localhost:8000/stop_profile    # always stop; a timed-out stop leaves the profiler running and slows the server ~2x until restart
```
TP=4 writes one trace per rank; analyze rank 0.

## Not applicable on gfx908

- FP8/MXFP4 findings and the "Peak MAF (FP8)" line: no hardware support.
- Suggestions to adopt CK flash-attention v3, hipBLASLt FP8, AITER CK MoE/sorting or `row_newbroadcast` DPP paths: these
  don't compile or don't exist for gfx908 (CK v3 uses `v_pk_mul_f32`, gfx90a+). Attention on our serves is
  `TRITON_ATTN` (or `ROCM_ATTN` for DeepSeek-OCR-2); always set it explicitly — auto-select picks the slow one.
- Graph-fork / multi-stream suggestions: HIP-graph sibling nodes drop the decode graph to a slow dispatcher on gfx908
  (+2.9 ms/step measured). Keep decode graphs linear.

## Hardware facts (shared across our MI100 skills)

**Fan rule (our chassis; check yours):** the case fans follow GPU0's temperature only. A single-card bench or job runs on **GPU0**, or another job keeps GPU0 continuously loaded; otherwise the fans stay at idle speed and the loaded card reaches 95 °C junction within minutes (2026-10-07: GPU1 alone, GPU0 idle, hit 95 °C at both 200 W and 290 W; that was the fan artifact, not a card limit). For capability numbers, load all four cards at once (most realistic) and log junction for every card.

gfx908 CDNA1, wave64, 120 CUs, 32 GB HBM2 per card. 4 cards, all-to-all xGMI, 1 hop (`rocm-smi --showtopo`, verified
2026-10-07). Power cap 200 W per card by policy (set with `rocm-smi --setpoweroverdrive` (we use a small sudo wrapper); 150 W = best
tok/s per W, 290 W only helps c≥16). Device-index traps (our host; check yours): rocm-smi GPU0..3 = renderD130, 131, 128, 129 (not in order);
an extra NVIDIA card sits at renderD132 — pass render nodes explicitly instead of a `/dev/dri/renderD*` glob; never set both
`HIP_VISIBLE_DEVICES` and `ROCR_VISIBLE_DEVICES` (they compose, and a mismatched pair leaves zero GPUs). Images keep
`HSA_OVERRIDE_GFX_VERSION=9.0.8` by convention.
