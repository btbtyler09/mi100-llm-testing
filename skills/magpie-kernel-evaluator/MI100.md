# Magpie on MI100 (gfx908) — MI100 addendum

Read this before SKILL.md/reference.md on a gfx908 host. It overrides conflicting guidance. Status tags:
**[measured]** on our cards, **[spec]** vendor sheet, **[unverified]** not tested here.

**Magpie itself has never been run on this node [unverified].** gfx908 is unlikely to be in its compatibility matrix;
treat `magpie --gpu-info`, the rocprof-compute/Metrix profiler path and `atom`/`sglang` workloads as unverified until a
smoke run passes. What follows is what Magpie should be pointed at and calibrated against.

## Benchmark target: our vLLM fork, not upstream

Upstream vLLM wheels/images take paths that crash or crawl on gfx908 (FP8 MoE, CK FA, wvSplitK skinny variants,
auto-selected attention). Benchmark with our images in `docker` run mode:

| image | use |
|---|---|
| `btbtyler09/vllm-rocm-gfx908:v0.28.0rc10.dev-q38fn` (digest 36fdc69f645b) | current base for all serves |
| `btbtyler09/vllm-rocm-gfx908:v0.28.0rc10.dev-q38fn-mtp` | Flash-Next MTP release (branch `qwen38-flash-next-mtp`, serve script `scripts/gfx908/serve_qwen38_flash_next_mtp.sh`) |

Required on every run: `--attention-backend TRITON_ATTN` (auto-select picks ROCM_ATTN, ~10x off the KV byte floor at
depth); `HSA_OVERRIDE_GFX_VERSION=9.0.8`; explicit render nodes 128–131; TP=4 for 27B+ models. Dense GPTQ-4bit:
`VLLM_DISABLED_KERNELS=ConchLinearKernel,TritonW4A16LinearKernel` (Exllama "dual" path; Triton W4A16 is picked by default
and is 2.2x slower at c=1 [measured, 30B dense GPTQ-4bit]). GPTQ checkpoints on Qwen3.5-family need `--dtype half`.

Workload rules from our campaigns (they override "compare TTFT/TPOT" defaults):
- Our quotable suite is `BenchAndReport.py` (this repo) 12-tier (run inside the container). Quick probes are
  debug signals, not results.
- Spec-decode arms only on real text, and gate on acceptance counters, never on coherence samples.
- Every perf boot needs a greedy-parity/coherence line; high tok/s with garbage output has happened.
- Boot-to-boot noise 0.3–0.4 ms/step: ≥3 boots per arm or in-process A/B before claiming < 0.5 ms/step.
- Server `--max-num-seqs` is headroom; the client sets concurrency. Don't lower max-num-seqs to "fix" a result.
- Thermals: single-card kernel benches run on GPU0 (the fans follow its temperature) or with GPU0 kept loaded; a bench on
  GPU1–3 with GPU0 idle measures a fan artifact (throttling at 95 °C junction), not the card. See the fan rule below.

## Trace + roofline (TraceLens post-processing)

Pass an explicit `gpu_arch_config` — Magpie's auto-detection has no gfx908 entry [unverified]. Use the MI100 constants in
`../tracelens-analysis-orchestrator/MI100.md` (**measured**, 4 cards sustained at stock 290 W: HBM 926 GB/s, fp16 123.7 /
bf16 65.8 / fp32 34.3 TFLOPS, int8 88.0 TOPS; at our 200 W production cap fp16 97.8, bf16 52.7; no FP8 key; use rocBLAS,
hipBLASLt runs at ~10% of spec on gfx908). Torch-trace capture that works on our fork:
`--profiler-config '{"profiler":"torch","torch_profiler_dir":"/prof"}'` + `POST /start_profile` / `/stop_profile`
around a c=1 decode window. The kineto profiler inflates small kernels ~1.3 → 3.75 µs on gfx908, so rank by measured
step deltas, not traced durations under ~30 µs.

## Kernel analyze/compare on gfx908

- HIP kernels JIT through `torch.utils.cpp_extension` fine on gfx908 (`--offload-arch=gfx908`); use content-hashed build
  dirs — torch's loader handed back a stale .so after a failed rebuild once (silent wrong A/B).
- Microbench small kernels inside a CUDA graph, HBM-cold; isolated tiny-kernel floor ≈ 1.3 µs [measured].
- Code-object size is a per-dispatch cost: linking extra variants into a hot extension cost 1–2 µs/dispatch [measured].
- Counter path that worked: rocprof counters (MemUnitBusy / VALUBusy) + a pure-read calibration kernel (1042 GB/s) +
  bytes-in-flight arithmetic. rocprof-compute (Omniperf) on gfx908 [unverified].
- Correctness gate before perf: unit test + logprob parity vs HF at production TP. A Triton BLOCK_M=8 QSA kernel
  miscompiled on gfx908 and still produced coherent text.

What works / doesn't at kernel level on gfx908 (our aiter fork):
- Works: AITER Triton GEMM, Triton MoE (E2E), paged-attention decode/prefill, RoPE, RMSNorm/LayerNorm, softmax;
  CK int8 W8A8 rowwise GEMM (`module_gemm_a8w8`, per-token x/w fp32 scales, on a not-yet-published aiter branch: meanrel 1.8e-4, 299 gfx908 shapes tuned, 1.37x geo-mean decode at kernel level, ~1.02x eager E2E).
- Doesn't: CK FP8/FP4 anything; CK FA v3 and other CK pipelines using `v_pk_mul_f32` (gfx90a+); CK moe_sorting (needs
  DPP `row_newbroadcast`, gfx90a+); AITER custom all-reduce (`AITER_CUSTOM_AR` defaults 0 — output corruption on gfx908).
  The live TP all-reduce on our serves is vLLM's custom AR (prove it from the boot log line `TP all-reduce …`).
- Our decode kernels (fork flags, all env-gated): push all-reduce over xGMI `VLLM_GFX908_PUSH_AR` (5.3 vs 7.8 µs per AR),
  W8A16 / W4A8 HIP GEMVs, fused GDN/QSA/HC glue, exact radix sampler. (flags documented on the fork). `VLLM_GFX908_CUSTOM_AR_MAX_SIZE_MB` caps the custom
  AR message size (larger messages go to RCCL); 64 MB vs default was neutral on a 30B dense GPTQ-4bit model (c=1 16.0 vs 15.7 ms TPOT, c=64 equal).

## Hardware facts (shared across our MI100 skills)

**Fan rule (our chassis; check yours):** the case fans follow GPU0's temperature only. A single-card bench or job runs on **GPU0**, or another job keeps GPU0 continuously loaded; otherwise the fans stay at idle speed and the loaded card reaches 95 °C junction within minutes (2026-10-07: GPU1 alone, GPU0 idle, hit 95 °C at both 200 W and 290 W; that was the fan artifact, not a card limit). For capability numbers, load all four cards at once (most realistic) and log junction for every card.

gfx908 CDNA1, wave64, 120 CUs, 32 GB HBM2 per card. 4 cards, all-to-all xGMI, 1 hop (verified 2026-10-07). 200 W cap
by policy (set with `rocm-smi --setpoweroverdrive`; restore 200 after any test). Below 200 W the cap clips clocks even
at ~50 W average draw (c=1 −6% at 150 W, −28% at 100 W). Device traps (our host): rocm-smi GPU0..3 = renderD130, 131, 128, 129;
an extra NVIDIA card sits at renderD132; never set both `HIP_VISIBLE_DEVICES` and `ROCR_VISIBLE_DEVICES`. Known host issue: RCCL on
the GPU2–GPU3 pair alone was broken in a DP probe [measured once; re-check before relying on 2-card groups].
